# What if everyone became obese overnight? (30 s YouTube Short)

1080×1920, 30 fps, 900 frames. Pip and Mo from the 2D demo, rebuilt in 3D: cel-shaded, ink outlines, the demo's
dusk light (a low warm sun, purple sky). The format is "Day N" beats, with captions in the top-left: a big "Day N",
a mono stat, a status pill and a line of text. The tone is dark and absurd: the world falls apart in stupid ways.
The jokes are about the world's reaction, never about anyone's body.

**The one fact (Day 14):** 8.1 billion people × about 30 kg each = 2.4 × 10¹¹ kg, which is about 240 million
tonnes. Earth's mass is 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg, so 2.4 × 10¹¹ / 5.97 × 10²⁴ = 4 × 10⁻¹⁴, which is 0.000000000004%.

**Sound:** a bass-heavy, slightly unhinged music bed, and a cartoon impact on every hit: the inflate, the chair, the
bonks, the floor.
