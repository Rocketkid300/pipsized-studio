## Numbers (worked out)

- **People**: 8.2 billion (UN World Population Prospects 2024: 8.16 bn mid-2024). The title rounds to "8 billion"; all
  maths below uses 8.0–8.2 bn and the rounded answers hold for both.
- **Density**: 4 people per m² is a dense, shoulder-to-shoulder standing crowd (G. Keith Still, crowd science: 4–5/m²
  is "packed", above ~5/m² becomes dangerous). So we use 4.
- **Area**: 8.0e9 / 4 = 2.0e9 m² = **2,000 km²** (8.2e9 / 4 = 2,050 km²). Said "two thousand square kilometres".
- **Square side**: √2,000 = 44.7 km (√2,050 = 45.3 km) → **45 km**.
- **Tokyo**: Tokyo Metropolis covers 2,194 km² (Tokyo Metropolitan Government) → the crowd is **smaller than Tokyo**
  (2,000–2,050 < 2,194).
- **Weight**: Walpole et al. 2012 (BMC Public Health, "The weight of nations"): average adult 62 kg. About 25% of
  people are under 15 (UN WPP 2024); call children ~25 kg on average. 0.75×62 + 0.25×25 ≈ 53 kg per person.
  8.0e9 × 53 kg ≈ 424 Mt (8.2e9 → 435 Mt) → **"over four hundred million tonnes"** (rounded down).
- **Pyramids**: Great Pyramid of Giza ≈ 6 million tonnes (usual estimate 5.9–6.25 Mt). 424 / 6 ≈ 71 → **seventy**.
- **Horizon**: distance to the horizon ≈ √(2·R·h) with R = 6,371 km, h = 1.6 m (eye height): √(2·6.371e6·1.6) ≈
  4.5 km. From the middle of a 45 km square the nearest edge is 22.5 km away → **the edge is past the horizon**
  (stat: 22 KM TO THE EDGE; the horizon is at 4.5 km).
- **Mexican wave**: Farkas, Helbing & Vicsek, Nature 419 (2002): the wave moves at about 12 m/s (~20 seats/s).
  45,000 m / 12 m/s = 3,750 s ≈ 62 min → **about an hour**.
- **Walking**: 45 km at a normal 5 km/h = **9 hours** (in practice slower; we say nine).
- **Land**: Earth's land area ≈ 148.9 million km². 2,000 / 148.9e6 = 0.0013% → **0.001%** (rounded down;
  it is one 75,000th). The rest of the planet is empty.
- **Ovens**: the joke. Not a statistic.

## Notes

- Hook is a flash-forward of the crowd: you are in it, and someone's on your foot.
- Humour is aimed at the situation (crowding, queues, ovens), not at any group.
