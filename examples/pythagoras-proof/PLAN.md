# Pythagoras: rearrangement proof (15 s, silent)

1920×1080, 30 fps, 450 frames. Matte black (#0E0E10), white strokes, colour-coded
quantities: **a = cyan**, **b = green**, **c = yellow**. The figure lives in the
middle band (y 220–860). The top band holds the title and the bottom band holds the equation.

| # | time | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5 s | Title fades in at the top. One right triangle draws its edges, and the labels a, b, c fade in. |
| 2 | 2.5–5.5 s | An outer square of side a + b draws. Four copies of the triangle fill its corners, and the tilted square left in the middle fills yellow and is labelled c². Caption: "area = c²". |
| 3 | 5.5–6.5 s | Hold, so the viewer can read the picture. |
| 4 | 6.5–9.5 s | Three triangles slide (translations only, the same four triangles) into two rectangles. The leftover area becomes the a² (cyan) and b² (green) squares. |
| 5 | 9.5–12.0 s | Bottom band: "same four triangles, same big square", then the equation a² + b² = c² is written term by term in matching colours. |
| 6 | 12.0–15.0 s | The equation pulses once and holds. The last frame is a clean end card. |

Audio: none. The render is muted, so students can record their own voice-over.
